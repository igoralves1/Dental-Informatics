# Project Document: Development of Fauchard - A Specialized Generative AI for Dentistry

## 1. Introduction
### 1.1. Purpose of Fauchard
- **Efficiency and Accuracy**: The Fauchard AI model aims to enhance the precision of diagnoses, treatment plans, and patient outcomes in the dental field. By leveraging AI, we can reduce human error and streamline complex decision-making processes.
  - **Business Model**: Subscription-based software as a service (SaaS) model for dental clinics. Offer different tiers of service based on the size of the practice and the level of AI integration required.
  - **Possible Clients**: Private dental practices, dental chains, and public health dental services.
  - **Support Dentists**: Provide AI-driven assistance for clinical tasks, improving confidence and accuracy in diagnostics and treatment plans.
  - **Possible Clients**: General practitioners, specialists (orthodontists, periodontists), and dental educators.
  
#### 1.1.1 Dental Office Management Tasks
- **Advertisement**: Use AI to optimize online and offline advertising campaigns for dental offices.
  - **Business Model**: Offer a pay-per-click or subscription service where dental practices pay for targeted advertising campaigns managed by AI.
  - **Possible Clients**: Individual dental practices, dental chains.
- **Web Traffic**: Analyze and optimize web traffic to increase patient bookings.
  - **Business Model**: Provide a monthly subscription service that includes SEO optimization, website analytics, and performance reports.
  - **Possible Clients**: Dental offices with an online presence.
- **Accountant**: Automate accounting tasks such as billing, invoicing, and payroll.
  - **Business Model**: Offer a subscription-based financial management service tailored for dental practices.
  - **Possible Clients**: Small to medium-sized dental offices.
- **Logistics Materials**: Manage inventory and logistics for dental materials.
  - **Business Model**: Integrate an inventory management system with AI capabilities into the SaaS offering, including automated ordering and restocking services.
  - **Possible Clients**: Dental practices, dental supply stores.
- **Connecting with Possible Dental Material Sales**: Facilitate connections between dental practices and suppliers.
  - **Business Model**: Commission-based model where the AI platform earns a percentage of sales made through its recommendations and integrations.
  - **Possible Clients**: Dental supply stores, dental material manufacturers.
